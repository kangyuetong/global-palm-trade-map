How to read this atlas

A map of commercial relationships, not shipping lanes.

01 / Trade lines
Curved lines connect Indonesia to five mapped partner countries. They are schematic, not actual shipping routes.

02 / Line thickness
Width reflects reported net weight where available. Missing weights are not estimated.

03 / Market rankings
The chart ranks all partners in the embedded dataset by export value. Some top-ranked markets may not have map markers.

04 / Time comparison
2024 is the only verified year embedded. Import additional verified years using the CSV control to enable comparisons.
DATA & METHODOLOGY · UN Comtrade, Indonesia as reporting country, exports of palm oil and its fractions (HS 1511), reference year 2024. Values are reported in US dollars and net weights in metric tonnes where available. Rankings reflect the supplied partner-level records; do not treat sums as an independently verified national aggregate. Data downloaded 10 November 2026. The map displays only five geocoded destinations; country positions and curved routes are schematic.
